# Payment (Pembayaran)

> Teaching data for a fictional store. Not a real company policy.

## Payment methods
TokoKita accepts bank transfer (virtual account BCA, Mandiri, BNI, BRI), e-wallets (GoPay, OVO, DANA, ShopeePay), QRIS and cash on delivery (COD).
Kata kunci: metode pembayaran, cara bayar, transfer bank, e-wallet, QRIS.

## Cash on delivery (COD)
COD is available for orders up to Rp 1.000.000. The customer pays the courier in cash when the package arrives.
COD coverage depends on the courier and changes often, so the AI agent cannot confirm it for a specific city or address. Shipping to an area does not mean COD is available there. If the address is covered, the COD option appears at checkout; otherwise a human agent can confirm.
Kata kunci: COD, bayar di tempat, bayar tunai.

## Payment deadline
Virtual account and QRIS payments must be completed within 24 hours. Unpaid orders are cancelled automatically.
Kata kunci: batas pembayaran, pesanan dibatalkan, belum bayar.

## Invoices
An electronic invoice is sent to the customer's email after payment. Company invoices (faktur) can be requested from a human agent.
Kata kunci: invoice, faktur, bukti pembayaran.
