# Returns and refunds (Pengembalian dan refund)

> Teaching data for a fictional store. Not a real company policy.

## Return window
Customers can request a return within 7 days after the package is delivered. The item must be unused and in its original box, with all accessories.
Kata kunci: retur, pengembalian barang, tukar barang, batas waktu retur.

## Damaged or wrong item
If the item arrives damaged or is not what was ordered, report it within 2 days of delivery with a photo or an unboxing video. TokoKita pays the return shipping in this case.
Kata kunci: barang rusak, barang salah, video unboxing, komplain.

## How to request a return
Returns and refunds are handled by a human agent. The AI agent creates a handoff ticket, and a human agent replies within 1 business day.
Kata kunci: cara retur, ajukan pengembalian, minta refund.

## Refund method and time
After the returned item is checked, the refund goes back to the original payment method within 3-7 business days. COD orders are refunded by bank transfer.
Kata kunci: refund, uang kembali, pengembalian dana, berapa lama refund.

## Items that cannot be returned
Opened earphones (for hygiene reasons) and items bought in clearance sales cannot be returned unless they are damaged.
Kata kunci: tidak bisa diretur, earphone, barang obral.
