# Shipping (Pengiriman)

> Teaching data for a fictional store. Not a real company policy.

## Shipping areas and couriers
TokoKita ships from our warehouse in Jakarta to all provinces in Indonesia. Couriers: JNE, J&T Express and SiCepat.
Kata kunci: pengiriman, kurir, ekspedisi, kirim ke daerah.

## Delivery time
Orders paid before 14:00 WIB are shipped the same day (Monday to Saturday). Estimated delivery: Jabodetabek 1-2 days, Java 2-4 days, outside Java 3-7 days. These are estimates, not guarantees.
Kata kunci: berapa lama sampai, estimasi pengiriman, kapan dikirim.

## Shipping fee
Shipping is free (gratis ongkir) for orders of Rp 150.000 or more. Below that, the fee is calculated at checkout based on the courier and destination.
Kata kunci: ongkir, ongkos kirim, gratis ongkir, biaya pengiriman.

## Tracking
After shipping, the tracking number (nomor resi) appears in the order details. Customers can also ask the agent with their order ID and the last 4 digits of their phone number.
Kata kunci: lacak paket, cek resi, nomor resi, tracking.

## Address changes
The delivery address can be changed only before the order is shipped. After shipping, contact a human agent.
Kata kunci: ganti alamat, ubah alamat, salah alamat.
