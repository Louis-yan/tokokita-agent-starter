# Store information (Info toko)

> Teaching data for a fictional store. Not a real company policy.

## About TokoKita
TokoKita is an online store in Jakarta selling phone accessories: power banks, charging cables, wall chargers and earphones. Products are imported from Shenzhen, China, through a licensed Indonesian importer.
Kata kunci: tentang toko, produk apa saja, asal barang.

## Customer service hours
The AI agent Sari answers 24 hours a day. Human agents work Monday to Saturday, 08:00-20:00 WIB.
Kata kunci: jam operasional, jam kerja CS, hubungi admin.

## Product authenticity and certification
All power banks sold by TokoKita carry SNI certification, handled by our importer. Each product page lists the certificate number.
Kata kunci: original, asli, SNI, sertifikat.

## Fast charging
Fast charging (up to 22.5W) works only with a USB-C PD cable and a phone that supports fast charging. Using two ports at the same time lowers the charging speed.
Kata kunci: fast charging, pengisian cepat, kabel PD, lambat ngecas.
