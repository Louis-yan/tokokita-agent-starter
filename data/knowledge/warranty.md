# Warranty (Garansi)

> Teaching data for a fictional store. Not a real company policy.

## Warranty period
Power banks and chargers have a 6-month warranty. Cables and earphones have a 1-month warranty. The warranty starts on the delivery date.
Kata kunci: garansi, masa garansi, berapa lama garansi.

## What the warranty covers
The warranty covers manufacturing defects, for example a power bank that does not charge or a broken indicator light. It does not cover damage from drops, water, or opening the device.
Kata kunci: klaim garansi, cacat produk, tidak bisa dicas, rusak.

## How to claim
Warranty claims are handled by a human agent. Prepare the order ID and a photo or video of the problem. The AI agent creates a handoff ticket.
Kata kunci: cara klaim garansi, servis.
